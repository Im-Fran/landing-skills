# Brief: Tallybook

- **Type:** SaaS
- **Language:** English
- **Product:** invoicing for freelance designers and developers. Create an
  invoice from a tracked project in two clicks, send it, get paid by card or
  bank transfer, and see which invoices are overdue.
- **Audience:** freelancers who currently invoice from a spreadsheet or a
  word-processor template.
- **Conversion action:** start a 14-day free trial. No card required.
- **Pricing:** one plan, 9 USD per month, or 90 USD per year.
- **Proof available:** none yet. The product launched last month.
- **Assets available:** three product screenshots, described as: invoice
  editor, overdue list, payment confirmation. No logo beyond the wordmark.
- **Brand constraints:** none.
- **Stack:** plain HTML and CSS.
